When a tooth is damaged or missing, the goal is to restore comfortable, confident function — and to do it in the most conservative way that will last. We reach for the least invasive option that solves the problem, whether that's an inlay instead of a full crown, or a root canal to save a tooth another office might pull.

Our restorative care covers the full range: fillings, crowns, bridges, implants, dentures, and root canal therapy. Where a tooth can reasonably be saved, we'll work to save it. Where replacement is the right call, we'll help you weigh the options honestly.

You'll always leave understanding what was done, why, and how to keep the result healthy for years.
