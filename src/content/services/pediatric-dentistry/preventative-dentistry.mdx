## What it is

Preventative dentistry is the everyday care that keeps problems from starting. Through routine cleanings, exams, and friendly guidance, we help protect your child's teeth and catch small concerns early, before they turn into something bigger.

## Who it's for

Preventative care is for every child, from the first tooth through the teen years. Building good habits and comfortable dental visits early sets kids up for healthy smiles that last well into adulthood.

## What to expect

Visits are calm and unhurried. We gently clean the teeth, check for early signs of decay or gum concerns, and keep an eye on how the teeth and bite are developing. We take time to explain what we see in words that make sense to kids and to you.

Our approach is conservative and least-invasive first, so we focus on prevention and honest guidance rather than unnecessary treatment. We'll share easy, practical tips for brushing, flossing, and snacks that help keep your child's smile healthy between visits.
