Most of what we do for kids is quiet work. A cleaning, a look around, and a few minutes of talk about brushing and snacks. That's the point. Preventative care is how we find a soft spot in the enamel before it turns into a cavity that needs a filling.

## Why we see kids every six months

Children's mouths change fast. New teeth come in, baby teeth loosen, and the bite shifts as the jaw grows. A checkup about every six months lets us follow those changes and spot early decay or gum irritation while it's still easy to treat. Some kids do better on a different schedule, and we'll tell you if yours does.

The first visit can happen around the first birthday or when the first tooth shows up. Mostly it's a chance to meet us.

## Habits that matter between visits

Say your 5-year-old carries a cup of apple juice around all afternoon. Even if she brushes well at night, her teeth get a fresh dose of sugar with every sip. Moving the juice to mealtime and offering water the rest of the day often does more than a new toothbrush would.

Brushing twice a day and flossing once a day still do most of the work at home. We keep care conservative. If nothing needs fixing, we won't go looking for something to fix.
