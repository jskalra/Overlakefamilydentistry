## What it is

A cavity is a small area of decay in a tooth. A filling gently removes that decayed area and restores the tooth with a tooth-colored material, stopping the decay from spreading and helping the tooth stay healthy and comfortable.

## Who it's for

Cavities can happen at any age, and children's teeth are especially worth protecting because baby teeth guide the adult teeth into place. If a routine exam finds early decay, treating it sooner usually means a smaller, simpler filling.

## What to expect

We keep things calm and unhurried. We explain each step in words that make sense to kids, gently numb the area if needed, remove the decay, and place a tooth-colored filling that blends in naturally. Most visits are short, and we check that the bite feels comfortable before your child heads home.

We believe in conservative, tooth-preserving care, so we always aim to keep as much of the natural tooth as possible and recommend the least-invasive option that will do the job well. We'll also talk through easy habits at home that help prevent future cavities.
