## What it is

A sealant is a thin, protective coating applied to the chewing surfaces of the back teeth, where deep grooves make cleaning difficult. By sealing out food and bacteria, it adds an extra layer of defense against cavities in the spots that are hardest to reach with a toothbrush.

## Who it's for

Sealants are often a good fit for children and teens once their molars come in, since those newly erupted back teeth are especially prone to decay. During an exam we'll let you know honestly whether sealants make sense for your child's teeth.

## What to expect

The process is quick, gentle, and completely comfortable, with no drilling or numbing involved. We clean and dry the tooth, apply the sealant into the grooves, and let it harden, usually in just a few minutes per tooth.

As part of our conservative, prevention-focused approach, we see sealants as a simple, low-cost way to help avoid fillings down the road. We'll check them at routine visits and reapply if needed, while continuing to encourage good brushing and flossing at home.
