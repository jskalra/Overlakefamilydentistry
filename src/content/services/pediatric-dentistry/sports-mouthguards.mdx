## What it is

A sports mouthguard is a custom-fitted appliance that cushions the teeth, lips, and gums during physical activity. Made from an impression of your child's own teeth, it helps absorb impact and reduce the chance of injury to the mouth during sports and active play.

## Who it's for

A mouthguard is a smart idea for active kids and teens who play sports with contact or a risk of falls. Whether your child is on a team or just loves the skate park, a comfortable, well-fitting guard makes protection easy to wear.

## What to expect

We take a quick, comfortable impression or scan of your child's teeth and use it to craft a guard that fits snugly and lets them breathe and talk with ease. A custom fit tends to stay put better than a store-bought version, so kids are more likely to actually keep it in.

In keeping with our prevention-first approach, we see a good mouthguard as a simple way to help avoid dental injuries that would need more involved treatment later. We'll check the fit at routine visits and can remake the guard as your child grows or as their teeth change.
