A root canal treats infection or damage deep inside a tooth. When the soft pulp at the center becomes inflamed or infected, we clear it out, clean and seal the inner canals, and save the tooth so it doesn't have to be removed.

## Why it saves your tooth

Root canal therapy lets you keep your natural tooth, which is almost always better for your bite, your jawbone, and your long-term comfort than losing it. It also relieves the pain that comes from an infected or dying nerve.

## What to expect

The tooth is numbed before we begin, so the visit generally feels similar to having a filling placed. Afterward the tooth may be tender for a few days, and because a treated tooth can become more brittle, we often recommend a crown to protect and strengthen it.

If you're having lingering tooth pain, sensitivity, or swelling, we'll examine the tooth and explain your options clearly. A root canal is our tooth-preserving choice whenever saving the tooth is realistic.
