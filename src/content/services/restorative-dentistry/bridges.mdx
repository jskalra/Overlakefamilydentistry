A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by using the healthy teeth on either side of the gap as anchors. A replacement tooth is held in place between two crowns, "bridging" the space so you can chew and smile normally again.

## Who a bridge may suit

A bridge can be a good fit when the teeth next to a gap are strong enough to serve as supports, or when an implant isn't the right choice for your health or bone. Replacing a missing tooth also helps keep neighboring teeth from drifting and your bite from shifting over time.

## What to expect

In most cases, treatment takes two visits. At the first, we gently shape the supporting teeth, take an impression, and place a temporary bridge. At the second, we fit and adjust the finished bridge so it feels comfortable and blends with your other teeth.

Because a bridge involves reshaping the neighboring teeth, we only recommend one after talking through your alternatives, including implants and simply monitoring the space. Our goal is always the least-invasive option that truly meets your needs.
