Dentures are removable replacements for a full arch of missing teeth. They restore your ability to chew and speak and support the shape of your face and smile when natural teeth are no longer present.

## Who dentures may suit

Full dentures are an option when most or all of the teeth in an arch are missing or can't be saved. They can be made for the upper arch, the lower arch, or both, and are a time-tested, affordable way to replace a complete set of teeth.

## What to expect

We take impressions and measurements to shape dentures that fit your mouth and match your natural appearance. Once they're ready, we adjust them for comfort and show you how to wear, remove, and clean them. Expect a short adjustment period as you get used to eating and speaking, and know that gums change over time, so occasional relines or adjustments help keep the fit secure.

If you'd like a more stable option, we can discuss whether implant-supported dentures are a good fit. As always, we'll recommend only what's genuinely right for you.
