A partial denture replaces several missing teeth with a removable appliance that clasps onto your remaining natural teeth. It fills the gaps so you can chew and speak more comfortably while keeping the healthy teeth you still have.

## Who a partial may suit

A partial can be a good fit when you're missing more than one tooth but still have strong, healthy teeth in the same arch. It's often a more affordable and less invasive choice than replacing each tooth individually, and it can be a sensible step while you consider longer-term options.

## What to expect

We take impressions and measurements so your partial is shaped to fit your mouth and match your natural teeth. Once it's ready, we adjust it for comfort and show you how to place, remove, and clean it. A short adjustment period is normal as you get used to eating and speaking.

We'll always talk through the alternatives, including bridges and implants, so you can choose the approach that fits your health, your goals, and your budget.
