A dental implant is a small post, usually titanium, that is placed in the jawbone to act as a replacement tooth root. Once it has healed, a crown is attached on top, giving you a replacement tooth that looks, feels, and functions much like a natural one.

## Why patients choose implants

Unlike a bridge, an implant stands on its own and doesn't require reshaping the healthy teeth beside it. Because it replaces the root, it also helps preserve the surrounding jawbone and keeps neighboring teeth from drifting into the gap.

## What to expect

Treatment usually happens in stages. After the implant is placed, it needs a few months to fuse with the bone before the final crown is attached. We'll coordinate each step and keep you comfortable and informed throughout.

Implants are an excellent option for many people, but they aren't right for everyone. We'll review your bone, gums, and overall health, and we'll only recommend an implant when it's genuinely the best choice for you.
