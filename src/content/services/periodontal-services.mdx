Your gums are the foundation everything else rests on. When gum disease sets in, it can quietly threaten teeth that are otherwise perfectly healthy — which is why we watch for it closely and treat it early.

For many patients, catching gum inflammation early means a straightforward deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) can treat and even reverse it, no surgery required. We'll show you what we're seeing and coach you on the simple habits that keep gums healthy between visits.

As with all our care, we start conservatively — treating what needs treating, and nothing more.
