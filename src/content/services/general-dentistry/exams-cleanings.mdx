A routine exam and cleaning is the foundation of keeping your smile healthy. It gives us a regular chance to check your teeth and gums, remove buildup that brushing and flossing can't reach, and spot small issues while they're still simple and inexpensive to address.

## What to expect

During a cleaning, one of our hygienists gently removes plaque and tartar from your teeth and along the gumline, then polishes and reviews your home care. Your exam includes a look at your teeth, gums, and bite, along with an oral cancer screening. If X-rays are due, we'll take them only when they'll help us understand something we can't see directly.

## Who it's for

Regular exams and cleanings are for everyone — children, adults, and longtime patients alike. Even if your teeth feel fine, routine visits help us catch decay, gum changes, and wear before they become bigger concerns.

## Our approach

We believe in conservative, tooth-preserving care. If we notice something, we'll explain it clearly and recommend the most straightforward option first. Our aim is to help you keep your natural teeth healthy for the long run, with honest guidance and no pressure.
