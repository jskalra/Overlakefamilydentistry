The temporomandibular joints connect your lower jaw to your skull, and when they're irritated they can cause pain, clicking, and trouble opening or closing comfortably. TMJ concerns are common and often improve with simple, conservative care.

## Who it's for

You may benefit from an evaluation if you have ongoing jaw pain or tenderness, hear clicking or popping when you chew, notice your jaw catching or feeling stiff, or get frequent headaches that seem connected to your jaw.

## What to expect

We start by listening to your symptoms and examining how your jaw moves, how your teeth fit together, and whether grinding or clenching may be involved. From there we talk through what we're seeing and outline options, beginning with the gentlest approaches.

## Our approach

Our philosophy is least-invasive first. That often means a custom nightguard, home care such as gentle exercises and warm compresses, and small habit changes, giving these time to work before considering anything more involved. If your situation needs care beyond what we provide, we'll be honest about that and help you find the right next step.
