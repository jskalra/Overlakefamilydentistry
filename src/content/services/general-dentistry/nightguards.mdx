A nightguard is a custom-made appliance you wear while you sleep to protect your teeth from the effects of grinding and clenching, also known as bruxism. Many people clench or grind without realizing it, and over time that pressure can wear down enamel, cause sensitivity, and contribute to jaw discomfort.

## Who it's for

A nightguard may help if you wake with jaw soreness or headaches, notice worn or sensitive teeth, or have been told you grind in your sleep. It's also worth considering if you've invested in dental work you'd like to protect from that added stress.

## What to expect

We start by looking at how your teeth are wearing and talking through your symptoms. If a nightguard makes sense, we take an impression or scan of your teeth so your guard is made to fit your bite comfortably and stay in place. When it's ready, we'll check the fit and make any small adjustments so it feels right.

## Our approach

A nightguard is a simple, non-invasive way to protect your natural teeth, which fits our conservative philosophy. We'll only recommend one if we think it will genuinely help, and we'll keep an eye on the fit at your routine visits.
