Professional whitening gently lifts stains that build up on tooth enamel over the years, from coffee, tea, wine, and everyday wear. It's one of the simplest, least-invasive ways to freshen your smile because it works with your natural teeth rather than covering or reshaping them.

## Who it's a good fit for

Whitening tends to work best on healthy teeth with yellowish or generalized staining. It's a good option if you're happy with the shape and alignment of your teeth and simply want them brighter. Some discoloration — from certain medications, injury, or the natural aging of the tooth — responds less predictably, and we'll be honest with you about what to expect before you begin.

## What to expect

We start with a quick exam to make sure your teeth and gums are healthy and to identify the cause of any staining. From there we'll recommend the approach that fits you, whether that's an in-office treatment or custom take-home trays you use on your own schedule. Either way, we'll walk you through how to keep sensitivity to a minimum.

Our goal is a shade that looks natural and suits your face — not an artificial, over-whitened look. If whitening alone won't get you where you'd like to be, we'll say so and talk through other options rather than pushing treatment that won't deliver.
