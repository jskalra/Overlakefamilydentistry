Veneers are thin, custom-made shells of porcelain that fit over the front of your teeth to improve their shape, color, and overall appearance. They can address concerns like chips, worn edges, stubborn discoloration, small gaps, or teeth that look uneven.

## An honest approach to a bigger commitment

Veneers can be transformative, but they're also a longer-term commitment — most traditional veneers require gently reshaping the front of the tooth, and that change is permanent. Because we practice conservative, tooth-preserving dentistry, we'll always walk you through simpler options like whitening or bonding first, and only recommend veneers when they're truly the best way to reach your goals.

## Who it's a good fit for

Veneers tend to suit people who want to address several cosmetic concerns at once and have healthy teeth and gums to build on. If your enamel is thin or you have active gum issues or grinding, we'll address those first and talk through whether veneers still make sense.

## What to expect

We start with a conversation about what you'd like to change, followed by an exam to make sure your teeth and gums are ready. If veneers are the right path, we'll design them to complement your face and smile, prepare the teeth, and fit the finished porcelain at a later visit — checking the look and your bite before we bond them in place.
