Dental bonding uses a tooth-colored resin to repair and reshape teeth. It's a great option for minor concerns like a chipped or cracked tooth, small gaps, a rough edge, or discoloration in a single area. The resin is applied directly to the tooth, shaped to blend in, and hardened in place.

## One of the most conservative options we offer

Bonding fits our tooth-preserving philosophy well: it usually requires little or no removal of natural enamel, and it's often reversible. For many small cosmetic concerns, it's the least-invasive way to get a real improvement — which is why we frequently suggest it before more involved treatments like veneers or crowns.

## Who it's a good fit for

Bonding is well suited to small, localized fixes on healthy teeth. If you're looking to close a small gap, smooth a chipped edge, or improve a discolored spot, it's often an ideal starting point. For larger changes across several teeth or for areas under heavy chewing force, we'll talk honestly about whether bonding will hold up or whether another option would serve you better.

## What to expect

Most bonding is completed in a single, comfortable visit and usually doesn't require anesthetic. We'll match the resin to your natural tooth shade, shape it carefully, and harden it in place, then polish it so it blends with your smile. We'll also let you know how to keep it looking its best over time.
