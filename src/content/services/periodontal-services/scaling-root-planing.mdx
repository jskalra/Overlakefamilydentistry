Scaling and root planing is a thorough, below-the-gumline cleaning used to treat gum disease in its earlier stages. It's often called a "deep cleaning" because it reaches areas a routine cleaning can't. Our goal is straightforward: calm the infection, help your gums reattach to your teeth, and preserve the bone and tissue that keep your teeth stable.

## Who it's for

This treatment is typically recommended when plaque and tartar have built up beneath the gumline and your gums have started to pull away from your teeth, forming pockets that trap bacteria. Signs can include bleeding when you brush, tenderness, or persistent bad breath. We recommend it only when your gums genuinely need it. If a regular cleaning will do, that's what we'll suggest.

## What to expect

We begin by numbing the areas we're treating so you stay comfortable. Scaling removes hardened deposits from the tooth surface and just below the gumline, and root planing smooths the root surfaces so gum tissue can heal and reattach more easily. Depending on how much treatment is needed, this may be done in one visit or spread across a couple, often one section of the mouth at a time.

## Afterward

It's normal for your gums to feel a little tender for a few days, and mild sensitivity to hot or cold can follow. Most patients manage this easily with over-the-counter pain relief. Over the following weeks, healthy gums tend to feel firmer and bleed less.

Because gum disease is a long-term condition, keeping it in check comes down to consistent care. We'll usually set you up with a maintenance cleaning schedule and talk through simple habits at home that protect the results, so you can avoid more involved treatment down the road.
