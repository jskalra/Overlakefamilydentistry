Invisalign for Teens uses the same clear, removable aligner system as the adult treatment, with a few features tailored to younger patients. It's a discreet way to straighten teeth without the brackets and wires of traditional braces, which many teens appreciate through their school years.

## Built for teenage smiles

Because teenagers often still have teeth coming in, teen aligners are designed to accommodate erupting teeth. Many sets also include small wear indicators that gradually fade with use, giving both your teen and our team a simple way to see whether the aligners are being worn enough to stay on schedule.

## Who it's for

Clear aligners tend to work best for mild to moderate alignment and spacing concerns. Just as important as the teeth themselves is whether your teen is ready to wear the aligners consistently and keep track of them. We'll talk through all of this honestly during the evaluation and let you know if traditional braces or another approach would serve your teen better.

## What to expect

After an exam and digital scans, we map out a step-by-step plan and provide the aligners in sets. Your teen wears each set for the recommended time, removing them to eat and to brush and floss, then switches to the next. We check in periodically to confirm progress. Once treatment wraps up, a retainer helps hold the new smile in place. We're glad to take a look and give you a straightforward recommendation for your teen.
