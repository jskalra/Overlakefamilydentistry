Invisalign is a system of clear, removable aligners that gradually move your teeth into better alignment. Instead of metal brackets and wires, you wear a series of custom, nearly invisible trays, changing to the next set as your teeth respond. For many people it's a discreet way to address crowding, spacing, or minor bite concerns.

## Who it's for

Clear aligners are a good option for many teens and adults who want a less noticeable alternative to traditional braces. They tend to suit mild to moderate cases best. Some more complex bite or alignment problems are still better served by other approaches, and we'll tell you honestly if that's the case for you rather than steer you toward treatment you don't need.

## What to expect

We start with an exam and a conversation about your goals. If aligners are a good fit, we take digital scans or impressions and map out a step-by-step plan. You'll receive your aligners in sets and wear each one for the recommended time before moving to the next, with periodic check-ins so we can confirm things are tracking as planned.

Because the aligners are removable, you can take them out to eat and to brush and floss normally, which makes day-to-day care straightforward. The trade-off is that results depend on consistent wear, so the aligners need to stay in for most of the day and night.

Once your teeth are where they should be, we'll fit you with a retainer to help keep them there. If you're curious whether clear aligners make sense for your smile, we're happy to take a look and give you a candid recommendation.
