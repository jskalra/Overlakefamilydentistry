Good dentistry is mostly about prevention — and that's where general dentistry comes in. Regular exams and cleanings let us catch small issues while they're still small, before they turn into bigger, costlier problems.

At every visit we take our time: a thorough exam, a gentle cleaning, and an honest conversation about anything we see. If a tooth is fine to watch rather than treat, we'll say so. If something needs attention, we'll explain why and walk you through your options.

From routine care to nightguards and TMJ relief, general dentistry is the foundation of the long-term relationships we've built with Eastside families for decades.
